# Handover

## Delivered against scope
Map working capabilities to Jira stories and the committed sprint backlog.

## Not delivered
List requested items that remain in the product backlog and why they were not committed.

## Setup
Follow README: create venv, install requirements, run `python run.py`, then `pytest -q`.

## Known issues
Document open defects and assumptions discovered during the sprint.

## Configuration
Use `.env.example`; never place real credentials in GitHub.

## Recommended next sprint
Prioritise the highest-value remaining backlog items with a short rationale for each.
